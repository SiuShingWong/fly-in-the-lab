# Fly in the Lab

A day in a fly lab at WIMP, a made-up institute on the coast. Walk round the building, talk to people, tag along on experiments, play the bench games, and say hello to the alpacas.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox). The game is `index.html`; the bench games (PCR, western blot, imaging and the rest) are the small pages in `games/`, which the game opens. Keep the two together. It only needs the internet for its fonts; without them it still runs, with plainer lettering.

## Putting it on GitHub Pages (public: anyone with the link can play)

1. On github.com, make a new repository (for example `fly-in-the-lab`).
2. Upload everything in this folder, including the `games` folder: `index.html`, `games/`, `README.md`, `TERMS.md`, `LICENSE` and `.nojekyll`. ("Add file" → "Upload files". `.nojekyll` is hidden on a Mac: drag the whole folder in, or press Cmd+Shift+. in the upload window to show it.)
3. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under "Build and deployment", choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch **main**, folder **/ (root)**, and Save.
4. After a minute or two the page shows the address, like `https://<your-username>.github.io/fly-in-the-lab/`.

To update the game, upload the new `index.html` (and `games/`, if the bench games changed) over the old ones.

Progress is saved in each player's own browser, so everyone keeps their own game. To keep a copy, or move it to another computer, use Settings → Back up my progress, then Restore from a backup.

## Science credits

The researchers' chat series and the Break the Symmetry bench game are based on these published papers. The characters are made up and talk about the papers as readers, not as their authors; the jokes and the player's lines are made up too.

- Ana1 (Katy's chats): Saurya et al. (2016), *Journal of Cell Science*. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4958303/
- Growing up (Kristina's chats): Novak et al. (2016), *Developmental Cell*. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4918730/
- The Spd-2 domain (Lucas's chats): Hu, Wainman et al. (2025), *Science Advances*. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11922060/
- Tags and shredders (Yannis's chats): *The EMBO Journal* (2025), PARP7, DTX2 and AHR. https://doi.org/10.1038/s44318-025-00656-1
- CDK at the pole (Liza's chats): Roberts et al. (2024), *Cell Reports*. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11554571/
- Break the Symmetry: Wilmott, Goriely & Raff (2023), *PLOS Biology*. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3002391

Journal club uses 29 more published papers on fly centrosomes and centrioles (2006 to 2026), mostly from the Raff lab. Each is credited on its title slide in the game and in the player's notebook.

The lab meeting talks are invented work in progress, written for the game; they are not real results.
