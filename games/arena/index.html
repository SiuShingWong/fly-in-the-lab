AlphaFold Arena

Ashley has the AlphaFold models of twenty proteins the lab works on. Pick a team of three and take on her teams, one on one, tagging in and out. Every fighter is built from its real model: folded domains (blue, high pLDDT) make it big, tough and slow, with short heavy hits; disordered regions (yellow and orange, low pLDDT) are whips that hit from far away, but stay out a moment after each lash, and a well-timed protease chops them off.

TanksMostly folded (Ana3, Msps, Polo, γ-tubulin): lots of HP and armour, heavy slams. Get in close.
WhippersLong disordered regions (TACC, Spd-2, Cep97, Ana1): hit from range, keep your distance. Every chop costs reach.
Tag pairsReal partners help each other when one tags the other in: Polo → Cnn or Spd-2, Aurora A → TACC, Plk4 → Ana2, TACC → Msps, Asl → Plk4.

Pick your team (3)

The first pick starts the fight. Ashley's tip: mix a tank and a whipper, and look for a real pair.
