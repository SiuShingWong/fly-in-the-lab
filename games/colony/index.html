Colony Picking Bench

Your HiFi assembly went into E. coli yesterday. Pick colonies, grow them overnight, then miniprep and sequence.

Tubes→Pick→Shaker→Next morning

Big, round, well-separated colonies are best. Keep the lid off as briefly as you can. About 4 minutes.

How each step works
  1. Get the tubes ready. 5 ml of LB in each, the right antibiotic for your plasmid, and a label on every tube.
  2. Pick single colonies. Take the lid off, touch one colony gently with a sterile tip, and drop the tip into a tube. Big, round, well-separated colonies are best. Watch out for the tiny satellite colonies around big ones, colonies touching each other, and anything that doesn't look like E. coli.
  3. Be quick. Every second the lid is off, things can fall in from the air. Put it back when you're done.
  4. Set the shaker. Temperature, speed, how long, and whether the caps are loose or tight.
  5. Next morning: look at the tubes, then miniprep and sequence. Some colonies will be empty vector however well you pick. That's why you pick more than one.
