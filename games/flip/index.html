Bench notes

Fly Vial Bench

Keep the lab's fly lines going: flip each stock into a fresh vial before the old one gets crowded and dries out.

Today: look after a tray of stocks for four weeks without losing a line. Flip when the young adults come out (green).
How it works
Watch them growEggs, larvae in the food, big larvae up the walls, pupae, then young adults: about 10 days at 25 °C.
Flip at the right timeClick a vial (or its Flip button) when the young adults are coming out (green). Crowded (amber) is late; drying out (red) is the last chance.
Use the 18 °C roomClick a vial's °C tag to move it. At 18 °C a generation takes about 19 days, so it needs flipping half as often.
