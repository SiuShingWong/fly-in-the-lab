1 · Break the needleTap the sealed tip against the coverslip edge. A gentle tap makes a small opening. A hard crash makes a wide one, which leaks and pushes embryos off the glue.
2 · Test the flowHold pressure in the oil and watch the droplet. No droplet means the tip is blocked, so re-crash (R) or put in a new needle (N).
3 · Line upDrag embryos from the tape onto the glue strip, keeping them on the line. Work fast, because they start drying the moment they're off the plate. Skip any with a clear, translucent edge: they're already past cycle 10 (Z magnifies). Check the glue first. Too thin and embryos slide off. Too thick and they sink unevenly. G adds heptane, V opens a new vial.
4 · DesiccateTop up the drying in the chamber if you need to, and stop when they look slightly baggy. Full, tight embryos push liquid back out. Shrivelled ones won't develop.
5 · Inject before the window closesLine up, go in, deliver a small dose, come out. If you overfill, E sucks some back. Never move the stage with the needle inside.
6 · Develop & imagePick an incubation time so that as many embryos as possible reach cycle 11 (about 1.5 h after laying) when you image them.
