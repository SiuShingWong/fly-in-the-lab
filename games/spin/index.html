Bench notes

Centrifuge Bench

An unbalanced centrifuge shakes, stops itself, and can damage the rotor. Five rounds: each time, load your tubes so the rotor is balanced, then spin.

Place the tubes→Balance tube if needed→Spin
How it works
Place your tubesPick a tube, then click a place in the rotor. Click a loaded place to take the tube out again.
Balance tubesIf your samples can't balance on their own, weigh out a tube of water on the scale to the right weight and add it.
SpinClose the lid. Balanced: it runs smoothly. Not balanced: it shakes and stops, and shows you where the weight is.

The rotors and the number of tubes change every time. About 5 minutes.
