1 · DechorionateBrush embryos off the juice plate, then bleach away the tough outer shell (the chorion). Not too short, not too long.
2 · Fix and devitelliniseChoose a fixative, then shake hard so the vitelline membrane pops off. Take the heptane off, wash three times in fresh methanol, and store them at 4 °C for at least a day. Then take only the embryos that sank.
3 · Rehydrate and blockOut of methanol into buffer, then block the sticky sites so antibodies bind only where they should.
4 · Primary antibodiesOne for centrosomes, one for microtubules. Watch the host species on the labels.
5 · SecondariesEach secondary finds one host species and carries one dye. Then wash, and add Hoechst for DNA.
6 · Mount and imageMount, then look at every channel and the merge on the microscope.
