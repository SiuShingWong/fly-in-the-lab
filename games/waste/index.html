Waste Bench

Recycling only works if what goes in is right: one syringe or a bit of agar spoils the bag.

Today: 3 questions from the posters on island C, then the end of a busy day. Put each thing in the right bin before it reaches the end of the bench.
The posters→Clear the bench

Keys 1–5 for the bins · E empty it first · K keep it. Or drag it. About 4 minutes.

The bins
1 · Soft plasticsClean wrappers, sleeves and soft packaging. Plastics 2 (HDPE) and 4 (LDPE).
2 · Lab plasticsTips, tubes, plates, flasks, plastic pipettes, Petri dishes, bottles. Contaminated or not; rinsed if needed. Plastics 2, 4, 5 (PP) and 6 (PS).
3 · Sharps and glassNeedles, syringes, blades, glass slides and coverslips, glass Pasteur pipettes.
4 · The normal routeWhat the posters say no to: gloves, paper tissues, agar and gels, plastic stuck to glass or metal.
5 · Paper and cardClean paper and cardboard: old printouts, delivery boxes.
E · Empty it first · K · Keep itLiquid left inside? Empty it into the liquid waste (E), then bin it. Some things aren't waste at all: wash them and put them back (K).
